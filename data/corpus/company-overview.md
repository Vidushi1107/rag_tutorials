# Helix Robotics — Company Overview

Helix Robotics B.V. was founded in 2019 in Rotterdam, Netherlands, by Mira Okonjo and
Devrim Aslan. The company designs autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for warehouse and
light-industrial environments.

## At a glance

- **Headquarters:** Rotterdam, Netherlands
- **Founded:** March 2019
- **Employees:** 340 (as of Q4 2025)
- **CEO:** Mira Okonjo
- **CTO:** Devrim Aslan
- **Revenue (FY2025):** €61.4 million
- **Offices:** Rotterdam (HQ), Katowice (manufacturing), Austin (North America sales)

## Product lines

Helix ships three product families:

1. **Atlas** — heavy-payload transport robots for pallet and cage movement.
2. **Scout** — lightweight inventory scanning and cycle-counting robots.
3. **Beacon** — fixed infrastructure (charging docks, traffic-control gateways, floor markers)
   that the Atlas and Scout fleets depend on.

The Beacon line is not sold standalone; it is bundled with any fleet deployment of four or
more robots.

## Fleet management software

All Helix robots are managed through **HelixOS**, a browser-based fleet controller. HelixOS
handles task assignment, traffic deconfliction, battery scheduling, and telemetry. A HelixOS
license is included for the first 24 months with every robot purchase, after which it renews
at €1,200 per robot per year.

HelixOS release 4.x is the current supported major version. Versions 3.x reached end of
support on 30 June 2025.

## Support model

Helix offers two support tiers:

- **Standard** — included with purchase. Business-hours email support, 2 business day response.
- **HelixCare+** — paid add-on. 24/7 phone and email, 4-hour response target, extended warranty,
  and two on-site preventive maintenance visits per year.

Support is delivered from Rotterdam for EMEA customers and from Austin for North and South
American customers.
