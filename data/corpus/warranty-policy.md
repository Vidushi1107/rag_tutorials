# Helix Robotics — Warranty Policy

Effective 1 January 2025. This policy supersedes all prior warranty terms.

## Coverage periods

| Product | Standard warranty | With HelixCare+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atlas R5 / R5-C | 24 months | 36 months |
| Scout M2 | 24 months | 36 months |
| Beacon docks and gateways | 12 months | 24 months |
| Batteries | 12 months or 1,200 cycles, whichever comes first | 24 months or 2,400 cycles |

Warranty begins on the date of commissioning sign-off, not the date of shipment. If
commissioning is delayed by more than 90 days after delivery for reasons attributable to the
customer, the warranty start date defaults to day 91 after delivery.

## What is covered

- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship
- Drive motors, gearboxes, and wheel assemblies under normal duty cycles
- LIDAR, camera, and sensor modules
- Charging electronics in both the robot and the Beacon dock
- Firmware defects that prevent normal operation

## What is not covered

- Damage from collisions caused by disabling or bypassing safety systems
- Operation outside the rated environmental envelope (for example, running a standard Atlas R5
  below -5 °C instead of the R5-C variant)
- Consumables: wheel treads, brushes, filters, and mast belts
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function
- Water ingress on Scout M2 units, which are only rated IP42 and must not be pressure-washed
- Third-party modifications, including non-Helix battery packs
- Sites where the floor flatness does not meet the DIN 15185 requirement stated in the
  deployment guide

## Making a claim (RMA process)

1. Open a ticket in HelixOS under **Support → New RMA**, or email `rma@helixrobotics.example`.
2. Include the robot serial number, the HelixOS incident ID, and a telemetry export covering
   the 24 hours before the fault.
3. Helix triages within 2 business days (Standard) or 4 hours (HelixCare+).
4. If the claim is approved, Helix ships a replacement module. The faulty module must be
   returned within 21 days or the replacement is invoiced at list price.
5. On-site repair is included under HelixCare+ only. Standard-tier customers are shipped
   parts for self-service or may purchase an on-site visit at €1,450 per day plus travel.

## Loaner units

HelixCare+ customers with fleets of 10 or more robots are entitled to a loaner unit if a
warranty repair is expected to exceed 5 business days. Loaner units are not offered under
the Standard tier.
